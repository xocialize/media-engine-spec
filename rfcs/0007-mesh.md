## Problem

Gaea will serve image → 3D (TRELLIS.2, `mlx-trellis2-swift` v0.10.0) next to Image, Audio and Video, and rigging
(SkinTokens, `mlx-skintokens-swift`) when its port lands. MES has no way to say any of it:

1. **No mesh kind.** Inputs and outputs are `text`, `image`, `mask`, `video`, `audio` or `data`. A GLB is none of them;
   as `data` it would carry no facts and no viewer would know what to do with it.
2. **No mesh facts.** A client can't tell a 30,000-face mesh from a 1.1-million-face one, or a textured mesh from a
   vertex-coloured one, without downloading and parsing it. Gaea's plan asks for vertex and face counts, the bounding
   box and the texture size.
3. **No conventions.** glTF says Y-up and metres but not where the object sits, how big it is, which way it faces, or
   which glTF features a client must be able to read. TRELLIS.2 exports Z-up by default, which lands every model on
   its side in a glTF viewer (the demo app's README).
4. **No warning for a degenerate result.** TRELLIS.2 rarely reconstructs a near-zero-thickness "card" from an image
   (MSS-9; AB-R-0059: a normal result's thinnest/longest extent is 0.43–0.57, the card's 0.0001). ModelSheet Studio
   guards it with min/max < 0.08. Nothing in a result says so.
5. **Required attribution.** TRELLIS.2 is MIT, but its DINOv3 conditioner's licence requires "Built with DINOv3"
   wherever it ships (`SW6-PACKAGING.md`: DINOv3 is the most restrictive component and governs). `license.notice`
   is "text the client MUST show", but nothing says the words must be shown exactly, and a client has no way to tell an
   attribution from a paragraph of terms it may summarize.
6. **Clients without a 3D viewer.** An agent, a web client, a notification: each needs a picture of the result.

## Proposal

### Base

**§6, §18: the `mesh` kind.** The kind registry gains `mesh`: a 3D asset, `model/gltf-binary` (GLB) in 0.1. Inputs,
outputs and adapter slots may be of kind `mesh`.

**§10.1 Results: mesh facts.** A `mesh` output reports:

| Fact | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `vertices` | Vertices, summed over every primitive (glTF's render vertices: a seam's duplicates count) |
| `faces` | Triangles, summed over every primitive |
| `bounds` | `{min: [x, y, z], max: [x, y, z]}`: the axis-aligned box of every vertex in scene space, in metres |
| `texture_size` | The longest side of the largest texture, in pixels. Present exactly when the mesh has a texture |
| `joints` | The skin's joint count. Present exactly when the mesh is skinned |

`vertices`, `faces` and `bounds` are REQUIRED on a `mesh` output. A profile says what else its outputs carry.

**§10.2 Licence: `attribution`.** The licence object gains:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `attribution` | OPTIONAL. Words the terms require shown with the model and what it makes, exactly as given (≤ 200 characters), e.g. `Built with DINOv3` |

- Rule 1 gains: a client MUST show an `attribution` verbatim (it may style it, never reword or translate it) wherever
  rule 1 has it show a notice, and in the MCP text summary, whatever else it shows. A licence may carry both: the
  attribution is the required words, the notice anything else the terms require shown.
- Rule 2 gains: the `attribution` goes into the file with the `spdx` and `notice`. And the table gains a row:
  GLB: `asset.copyright` (the attribution, then the `spdx`), and `asset.extras.mes` (`license`, `notice`,
  `attribution`).
- **Components.** A model built from parts under different terms states the governing licence (the most restrictive
  part's) as its `spdx`, and carries every part's required attribution in `attribution`, separated by "; ". TRELLIS.2:
  `LicenseRef-DINOv3`, `commercial_use: true`, `attribution: "Built with DINOv3"`.

**§12, §18: profile error reasons.** Profiles may register `reason` values, as they register warnings. (Mesh registers
`degenerate_result` with `input_invalid`.)

**§15 MCP.** A model line carries the offer's `attribution` ("attribution: Built with DINOv3"); a result's text summary
carries the attribution of every licence in its provenance. A `mesh` output gets a `resource_link` like any output; its
`preview` image is an image block like any image output.

### The Mesh profile 0.1

Written in full in [`spec/profiles/mesh.md`](../spec/profiles/mesh.md). In short:

- **`mesh.generate`**: `image` (one) → `mesh` (GLB) and `preview` (PNG). Parameters `seed`, `quality`, `resolution`,
  `max_faces`, `texture`, `texture_size`, `background`, `placement`, `size_m`, `on_flat`.
- **`mesh.rig`**: `mesh` (one GLB) → `mesh` (the same geometry with a skin) and `preview`. Parameters `skeleton` (a
  registered template, `vrm_humanoid` first; absent: the model's own), `seed`, `quality`. The input's vertices are kept
  exactly, in order: SkinTokens propagates weights onto the original vertices (AB-R-0057), so this costs it nothing.
- **Conventions**: glTF 2.0 binary, everything embedded, no `extensionsRequired`; triangles with normals; Y-up, metres,
  the front facing +Z; centred on the origin and fitted to a 1 m box unless `placement` and `size_m` say otherwise;
  PBR metallic-roughness with a texture atlas or vertex colours.
- **Every mesh output comes with a `preview`**: a square PNG of at least 512 px, the mesh from the front-right and
  above, on a transparent background.
- **Flat results**: an engine measures every result's thinnest extent against its longest; under 0.08 it warns
  `flat_result`, or with `on_flat: fail` fails the job 422 `input_invalid`, reason `degenerate_result`.
- **`supports`**: `background_removal`, `resolutions`, `textures`, `texture_sizes`, `maps`, `skeletons`,
  `auto_skeleton`, `max_influences`, `deterministic`. **Limits**: `min_faces`, `max_faces`, `max_texture_size`,
  `max_input_faces`, `max_joints`.
- **Disclosure**: what `generate` makes says so in the file (`asset.extras.mes.ai_generated`, and the preview's
  `Comment`); `rig` keeps the input's marking.
- **Not in 0.1**: several views of one object, a turntable video, compressed meshes (Draco, meshopt), USDZ, text → 3D,
  `mesh.edit` (TRELLIS.2's mesh → structured-latent encoder exists only for CUDA, AB-B-0027), animation.

## Compatibility

Additive while Base is 0.1:
- A new kind: older clients never see one, because only Mesh operations use it, and they don't know those
  operations. Clients MUST treat an unknown kind like `data` (a file to download), which Base §6 already implies.
- The mesh facts and `attribution` are optional fields. An older client ignores `attribution`, and still shows the
  `notice` where there is one, so an offer whose terms require attribution SHOULD also say so in its `notice` until its
  clients are on this release (Gaea's TRELLIS.2 plugin does).
- Profile error reasons: clients already treat unknown reasons as their code.

## Conformance

- **MESH-001 to MESH-012** (Mesh §6), for the operations an engine offers.
- **BASE-014 to BASE-019** run on Mesh operations too.

## Alternatives considered

- **3D as `data` or `image` outputs.** No facts, no viewer, and a GLB isn't an image.
- **A separate 3D server.** The operator's call: one Mac's memory under one scheduler; Gaea's plugin, trust, update,
  crash and spool machinery is profile-neutral.
- **`notice` alone for attribution.** A notice may be long and a client may paraphrase it; "Built with DINOv3" must
  appear as written. A separate field also lets a viewer put it under the result.
- **A `components` list of licences per offer.** Precise, but every policy rule would have to fold over it, and the
  fleet's packages already state the governing licence (MLXEngine's two-layer gate: C7 for the weights). The
  governing `spdx` plus the parts' attributions carries what clients must act on.
- **The preview as a `job.preview` event, or as an output fact.** Events are advisory and fact fields don't hold
  files; a client without a viewer needs the still in the result, with the same retention as the mesh.
- **USDZ.** Apple's viewers read it, but nothing in the fleet writes it and glTF is what generators emit; a USDZ
  `output` option can come later.
- **Refusing every flat result.** A coin or a sheet of paper is legitimately flat; the client decides with
  `on_flat`.

## Decision

Accepted on 2026-11-09, as proposed, with Mesh 0.1. Landed in MESKit v0.11.0:
- `Kind.mesh`, the mesh facts on `Output` and `ProducedOutput.mesh(…)`, `License.attribution` (MESCore); `GLB`, a
  reader that computes the facts and checks the conventions, and a writer (MESCore); `MeshProfile` and
  `MeshProfileRules` (gates, limits, 400s).
- `MockMeshEngine`: `mesh.generate` (a small icosphere coloured from the image, textured or vertex-coloured, flat on
  request) and `mesh.rig` (a `vrm_humanoid` or two-joint skin); MESH-001 to MESH-012.
- MESClientKit: `Output.isMesh`, `JobResult.preview(for:)`, `EngineConnection.downloadMesh`; MESFormsUI: `MeshView`
  (SceneKit, from MESCore's reader; the preview PNG when the file can't be read) and `MeshInputWell`; MESEngineUI shows
  an offer's attribution on its model card; `mes run` saves `.glb`.
